**`analysis_detect_key` dominates analysis-per-track wall-clock at 94.4% (mean 13.01s of 13.78s).** This confirms the Session 32 exploratory finding (7-track run, 94.4%) at higher N (24 tracks). Other inner analysis stages are negligible:

- `analysis_librosa_load`: 3.4% (audio decode)
- `analysis_detect_bpm`: 2.1%
- `analysis_read_tags`: 0.1%
- `analysis_db_update`: 0.0% (rounding error)

The brief's named optimisation (c) "share the librosa decode between BPM and key detection" caps at a 3.4% improvement to analysis-per-track wall-clock under ideal execution. The real budget should go to `key_detector.py` internals — chroma extraction method and Krumhansl-Schmuckler correlation.

**Ingestion is fast and conversion-bound.** Mean 257ms per file (median 230ms). ffmpeg conversion is 65.8% of that. Concurrent conversion re-enabling (brief's secondary deliverable, currently capped at `max_concurrent_conversions=1`) would parallelise this — the absolute savings are small in wall-clock terms but the architectural fix matters.

**XML export is sub-30ms whole-pipeline.** Not a candidate for optimisation work.
